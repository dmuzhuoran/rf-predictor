We established an end-to-end modeling pipeline that enables raw clinical variables with missing values to be directly used for subtype prediction. The workflow consisted of:
(1)stratified train–validation split;
(2)unified preprocessing including imputation, standardization, and feature encoding;
(3)principal component analysis (PCA) retaining ≥90% of cumulative variance;
(4)training a random forest classifier on the principal components with probability outputs;
(5)probability calibration on the validation set;
(6)combining calibrated probabilities with class centroids in the PC space through a probability–distance fusion scheme, with a rejection threshold of 0.70 for uncertain cases.
